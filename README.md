Whether you have a powerful NVIDIA gaming GPU or just want to use your computer's CPU, hMiner has a version for you. It's the perfect tool for beginners to start their crypto journey and a convenient utility for experts.

*   **Stunning Dark-Themed GUI:** An eye-friendly, modern interface that looks great and is easy to navigate.
*   **Dedicated GPU & CPU Versions:**
    *   **GPU Miner:** Unleash the full power of your **NVIDIA GPU** with our custom-built CUDA kernel for maximum hashrate (MH/s). Perfect for SHA-256 coins like Bitcoin Cash (BCH).

*   **System Tray Integration:** Runs quietly in your system tray, allowing you to show/hide the miner or start/stop mining with a simple right-click.
